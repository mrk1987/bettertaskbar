# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want BetterTaskbar to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of
experience level, background, identity or preferred operating system.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, reviews and discussions.
- Assume good intent and ask clarifying questions before escalating.
- Give actionable feedback about code, not about people.
- Respect maintainers' time: search existing issues first, keep reports
  reproducible and stay on topic.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks.
- Discriminatory language or imagery.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or deliberate bad-faith engagement.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues or block contributors who violate
this code. Decisions are made in the interest of a healthy project and are not
up for public debate.

Report concerns privately to the maintainer, [@mrk1987](https://github.com/mrk1987),
through GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially.

This code is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
